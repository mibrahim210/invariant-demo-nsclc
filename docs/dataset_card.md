# Dataset card - invariant-synthetic-nsclc-v1

Card version 2.0 | 25 September 2026 | Generator 1.1.0 | Protocol v2.0 (`docs/study_protocol.md`)

Release status and measured values are recorded in `docs/release_record.md`, which is generated from
the committed manifests by `scripts/write_release_record.py`. This card does not restate hashes or
metrics, so it never disagrees with them.

## Purpose and origin

A fully synthetic benchmark for testing ML-evaluation correctness, in particular patient-level
train/test independence. All patients, studies, identifiers, images and labels are fictional and
procedurally generated by `demo_repo/data_gen.py`. No real patient images, records, personal
information, client data, social-media data or public datasets are inputs. "NSCLC-inspired" describes
the fictional application context only.

The target is a simulated binary imaging phenotype. Neither class is a validated healthy or cancer
category. Do not use this benchmark for diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, clinical validation,
biological inference or claims of real-world model performance.

## Composition and format

- 200 patients, one study each, eight slices each: 1,600 samples.
- Exactly 100 patients per class; each patient's label is constant across its slices.
- One `.npy` file per slice: shape (2, 64, 64), C-order float32, finite values in [0, 1], loaded with
  `allow_pickle=False`.
- Channel 0 is CT-like and channel 1 is PET-like. Both derive from the same patient anatomy, texture
  grid, lesion center and per-slice translation. They are phantoms, not a 3D volume, real scans,
  Hounsfield-unit maps or SUV measurements.
- Filenames are identity-based (`NSCLC_P000_S00.npy`) and never encode labels.

## Metadata and artifacts

Columns: `slice_id, patient_id, study_id, slice_index, array_path, label, generator_version`.
`slice_id` is unique and becomes the DataFrame index; `array_path` is relative to the repository root.
Rows are sorted by `patient_id`, then `slice_index`.

## Generation and engineered correlations

Patient anatomy (body and lung ellipses with small center jitter) and a persistent 8x8 texture grid
are drawn independently of the label. A Gaussian lesion placed in one lung carries the phenotype
signal: class 1 lesions are larger and brighter, most strongly in the PET-like channel. Each slice adds
a small shared translation, a slice-position-dependent lesion scale and independent Gaussian noise
per channel. The exact numerical rules, masks, translation rule and RNG draw order are the `draw_order`,
`masks` and `translation_rule` fields of the configuration.

Disclosure: the persistent patient texture is deliberately engineered so that a model can recognize
individual patients across slices. Under a split that places slices of one patient in both training
and test sets, this produces optimistic metrics. The correlation exists to make that evaluation defect
measurable; it is not evidence about real NSCLC imaging.

## Evaluation

Evaluation follows the study protocol: two-way 80/20 splits, image-only features (8x8 block means,
128 features), a fixed distance-weighted 5-nearest-neighbor classifier, patient-level ROC AUC from
mean held-out slice probabilities as the primary metric and slice-level ROC AUC as secondary, seeds
0-4, all results retained and undefined metrics reported as unavailable.

## Quality checks

The generator refuses to overwrite existing outputs and runs only from a clean committed checkout.
`--check` regenerates into a temporary directory and compares metadata bytes and array hashes with
the committed manifests. Tests cover counts, identifier uniqueness, label consistency, shape, dtype,
range, manifest verification and determinism. These checks do not establish imaging realism or
clinical validity.

## Reproducibility

Dependencies are locked in `uv.lock` and the interpreter is pinned by `.python-version`. `.gitattributes`
forces LF line endings so hashes agree across operating systems. Regenerate arrays only from the frozen
generator and verify them against the manifest. The configuration is not edited after generation.
